# Shanti-Dweep-The-Sindhu-Accord — Constitution

> The non-negotiable rules. Every RFC, spec and pull request is checked against this. If a
> change conflicts with it, stop and say so — amend it deliberately instead of working around it.
> Keep it short: a rule that cannot be checked is a wish.

**Version:** 1.0.0 · **Ratified:** 2026-11-10

## Principles
1. Fiction, stated: the film opens with a card that reads "A work of fiction."
2. Unity is the theme: nothing hateful toward any religion or nation.
3. The owner approves each stage before the next begins: script, shot list, character designs
   and style frames, each act's render.
4. One timeline: every sound and picture event is timed in `score.js`, nowhere else.
5. Consistent characters: Bhaskar and Fatima, young and old, come from one shared design file.

## Guardrails (things that must never happen)
- No party logos.
- No blood, gore or on-screen killing. Violence is shown as aftermath or on news screens only.
- No realistic likeness of a real politician. Historic figures appear only as stylised silhouettes.
- No map of India that departs from the official Survey of India borders.
- No slur or demeaning portrayal of any faith, people or country.

## Quality gates (what "done" means)
- The delivered film is 1920x1080, 30 fps, about 12 minutes.
- English subtitles are in sync with the speech.
- Hindi and Urdu text has been spell-checked.
- The skill's `qa` and `verify` checks pass for each act, or each failure is listed as a known issue.
