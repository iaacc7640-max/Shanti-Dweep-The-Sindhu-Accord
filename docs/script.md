## Notes for the owner

1. **"MNP" is never expanded.** The brief does not say what the letters stand for, so the script only says "एम.एन.पी.". Tell me the full name if it should be spoken once.
2. **Lines I added** to carry the story (not in the brief): 1.8, 1.11–1.12, 2.3, 2.5, 2.12, 3.6, 3.8, 4.4, 4.9. Strike any you dislike.
3. **History lines to check before release:** 1.1–1.2 (Jinnah defending Tilak in the Bombay High Court, 1916) and 1.6 (the family's losses in 1948) are written from the brief and general knowledge. They were not checked against a source in this session.
4. **Safety choices.** The terror strike (3.3–3.4) is seen only on news screens. The crushed protests (3.2) are shown as aftermath. No leader of Pakistan, Bangladesh or China is named or drawn as a likeness. The union (4.12) keeps three nations, flags and constitutions.
5. **The oath (2.14)** is a shortened paraphrase, not the legal text of the Prime Minister's oath.
6. **Length.** About 900 Hindi and Urdu words. At a calm pace this fills roughly 7 of the 12 minutes; music, maps and silent shots fill the rest.
