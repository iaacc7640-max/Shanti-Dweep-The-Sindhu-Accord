# Shot list — Shanti Dweep: The Sindhu Accord

**Draft 1 — 2026-11-10 — awaiting owner approval.** 73 shots. Times are targets (±3 s per shot).
Look: watercolor. Flashbacks in sepia. "Rows" point to `docs/script.md`.
Camera words: *push* = slow zoom in · *pull* = slow zoom out · *pan* = slide sideways · *parallax* = near and far layers slide at different speeds · *hold* = still frame with small movement (water, light, breath).

Music themes: **BH** = Bhaskar (Konkan: bamboo flute, soft plucked strings) · **FA** = Fatima (Hindustani: harmonium, tabla) · **AC** = Accord (both together, with strings).

## Notes for the owner
1. **Honouring the four icons (1-14)** uses emblems and names, not faces, to keep the no-likeness rule.
2. **Leaders at the signing (4-10)** are seen from behind and far away; none is named.
3. **Running time** adds up to about 12:10 with credits.
4. **Maps.** The engine's built-in country outlines come from Natural Earth. India's northern outline will be redrawn to the Survey of India shape before any map shot is made.
5. **Violence.** 3-3, 3-4, 3-5, 3-9 and 3-17 are staged so that no person is shown being hurt.
