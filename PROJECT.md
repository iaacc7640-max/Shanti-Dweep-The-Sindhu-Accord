## What this is
"Shanti Dweep: The Sindhu Accord" is an animated short film of about 12 minutes. It is
speculative political fiction (a "What If"), not history. Two Bombay lawyers, Bhaskar Vishnu
Khare and Fatima Hussain, reshape the subcontinent and end with a peaceful union signed on the Indus.

## Who it is for
The Hindi-speaking general public on YouTube. Narration and dialogue are in Hindi with English
subtitles; Fatima's address to Pakistan is in Urdu, subtitled.

## Why now / what success looks like
A finished, watchable film whose theme is unity:
- one 16:9 1080p film of about 12 minutes,
- one vertical 9:16 teaser per act,
- English subtitles in sync, Hindi and Urdu spelled correctly,
- every safety rule in CONSTITUTION.md kept.

## Scope
**In scope:** script, shot list, character designs, style frames, the film, the teasers, subtitles,
a list of known issues.
**Explicitly out of scope:** publishing to YouTube; realistic likenesses of real politicians;
painted or AI-generated image frames (the owner chose the code-drawn watercolor look on 2026-11-10);
human voice recordings (the owner chose synthetic Piper voices on 2026-11-10).

## Glossary
- **Act** — one of the six parts of the film: Cold Open, Act I, Act II, Act III, Act IV, Epilogue.
- **Teaser** — a short vertical (9:16) cut for one act.
- **MNP** — the fictional party Bhaskar and Fatima found in the story.
- **Islam-e-Bharati** — the fictional reform movement they found in the story.
- **The Accord** — the fictional Sindhu Accord, the union treaty signed on the Indus.
- **Score** — the single timeline file that both the soundtrack and the pictures read.
